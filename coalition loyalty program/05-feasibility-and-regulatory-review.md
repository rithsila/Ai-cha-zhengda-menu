# Feasibility and Cambodia regulatory review

As of 9 September 2026. This is preliminary business research, not a Cambodian legal opinion. It identifies specific launch decisions; it does not establish an exemption, license eligibility, or regulator approval.

## Overall judgment

**Conditional go for discovery; not yet a go for public coin sales or prize draws.** The menu SaaS plus funded rewards concept is technically achievable, but demand, partner permissions, merchant economics, and operational execution remain unproven.

| Dimension | Assessment | Evidence or next proof |
|---|---|---|
| Customer familiarity | Positive signal | Existing local bank and chain rewards; see report 02. Another account may still be unwelcome. |
| Competitive space | Difficult | Direct network rewards and low-priced merchant software already exist. Need a specific neighborhood/workflow advantage. |
| Merchant willingness to pay | Unknown | Require conditional commitments at disclosed costs, not compliments about the idea. |
| Operator economics | Thin initially | Report 03's base case is slightly loss-making; spread-only requires substantially more volume. |
| Technical delivery | Feasible with material work | Existing menu/staff/loyalty foundation, but sampled schema lacks a coalition financial model. |
| Payments/top-ups | Unresolved and important | Multi-merchant purchased value needs classification and a suitable partner arrangement. |
| Prizes | Unresolved; heightened beverage issue | New 2026 beverage measures and sales-promotion requirements need review against the exact program. |
| Operational settlement | Critical | Must prove full funding, reconciliation, merchant payouts, reversals, and no duplicate spending. |

## 1. Payment regulation and funds custody

Cambodia's government fintech roadmap describes the 2017 payment-services framework as licensing persons providing payment services and addressing fund safety, AML/CFT, operational risk, and consumer protection. It is historical context; the current classification of this proposed coin product needs confirmation. The NBC maintains a current regulated payment-institution register. [Government roadmap](https://digitaleconomy.gov.kh/public/images/fintech/pdf/FinTech_Development_Roadmap_06012021_Final_06Jan2021_English_translation.pdf), [NBC register](https://www.nbc.gov.kh/english/supervision/payment_service.php)

**Inference for this project:** accepting money from customers and enabling value to be spent at unrelated merchants raises payment/stored-value questions. Calling the units “loyalty coins,” excluding cash-out, or using KHQR does not by itself answer them. Merchant-funded rewards with central settlement also need review; “earned only” is not asserted to be exempt.

Preferred investigation: a licensed bank/payment provider handles any regulated custody and payment services while the business provides menus, rewards records, and merchant operations under a reviewed contract. A provider's ordinary payment gateway contract may not cover this; obtain explicit product approval and division of responsibilities.

Ask a Cambodian lawyer and proposed provider for written answers on:

- Classification of earned reward units versus purchased units/prepaid balance.
- Who legally issues the value and owes the customer/merchant.
- Whether operator licensing, registration, approval, or an agency arrangement is needed.
- Permitted custody, safeguarding, settlement, refunds, KYC/AML, limits, and complaints handling.
- Whether the partner supports multi-merchant top-ups, split or batch payouts, confirmations, and insolvency/exit arrangements.
- Which current instruments amend the historical framework, and the actual cost/time to comply.

No definitive licensing capital requirement or implementation timeline is quoted here because this product's classification is not established.

## 2. Promotions, lucky draws, and the new beverage instruction

The GDT's official index lists **Instruction 011 MEF.S.N.N.GDT, dated 29 June 2026**, on alcoholic products and sugar-sweetened beverages as valid. The linked original is Khmer; this research does not claim a certified translation. [Official listing](https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/instruction/page/0), [original instruction](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/fcc8c691-5d3a-4fc8-aa5a-0644dcd9d25d)

KPMG's July 2026 interpretation describes restrictions for manufacturers, importers, and distributors, including packaging-linked prizes using QR/digital mechanisms, with cessation by 30 September 2026 and some earlier packaging restrictions. It also discusses free goods and limits on discounts/other benefits. **The application to freshly prepared café drinks, mixed restaurant purchases, or a separate network reward campaign is not resolved by this summary.** [KPMG analysis](https://kpmg.com/kh/en/insights/2026/07/implementation-of-measures-prohibiting-promotional-prize-schemes.html)

An earlier May government news report gives broader timing language. Treat the later instruction and a professional reading of its Khmer original as the basis for advice rather than merging the two reports into one assumed rule. [AKP report](https://www.akp.gov.kh/post/detail/369463)

Separately, Cambodian counsel's summary of Prakas 095, dated 12 April 2024, describes transparency, genuine rewards, Khmer advertising, and pre-publication advertising-document verification requirements. Confirm the actual procedure and its application to this launch. [VDB Loi analysis](https://www.vdb-loi.com/kh_publications/unfair-business-practices-in-advertising-and-sales-promotions/)

The National Trade Repository publishes the gambling prohibition subject to government permission, and a lottery-business licensing measure. These do not settle whether the founder's exact promotion is an ordinary sales promotion or regulated gambling. Do not import US “no purchase necessary” rules as a Cambodian safe harbor. [Gambling law](https://cambodiantr.gov.kh/en/document/?title=law-on-the-suppression-of-gambling), [lottery measure](https://cambodiantr.gov.kh/en/measure/?title=licensing-requirement-for-operating-a-lottery-business)

**Business consequence:** do not make phone prizes essential to customer value, sell chances, or advertise top-up-linked random draws until local counsel reviews the exact entry flow, participant ages, eligible products, promotional copy, prize procurement, draw process, and any required approvals. Also review deterministic drink rewards; removing randomness does not automatically resolve all beverage promotion questions.

## 3. Other business setup work

Have counsel/accountant determine applicable company and e-commerce registrations, tax registration, invoice treatment, currency/Khmer presentation, consumer terms, advertising submissions, personal-data obligations, vendor contracts, and treatment of reward liabilities/service income/prizes. These are work items, not a claim that a specific license or tax rate applies in every case.

Merchant agreements need clear rates, award funding, refunds, payout timing, dispute rights, fee changes, business closure, and customer protection. Customer terms need accurate qualification rules, reward availability, purchase refunds, expiry, account recovery, and support. Do not launch with copied foreign program terms.

## 4. Prioritized risk register

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation / evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Unfunded or misused reward balances | Critical | Coverage ledger, approved funds arrangement, daily reconciliation, separate operating budget; never rely on new deposits to pay old claims. |
| Top-ups classified as unauthorized payment service | Critical | Written classification and suitable provider agreement before enabling customer purchases. |
| Prize/beverage campaign outside permitted rules | Critical | Exact-flow legal review before announcement; keep it outside the initial proposition. |
| One shop can view another's data | Critical | Tenant authorization design and independent cross-tenant tests before onboarding unrelated businesses. |
| Invented sales or collusive redemptions | High | Verified receipts/provider references, prefunding, limits, audit logs, staff controls, and holds for anomalies. |
| Merchants fund others but see no benefit | High | Track net inflows/outflows and incremental visits; voluntary renewal and budget controls. |
| Rewards take too long to earn | High | Customer comprehension and time-to-first-reward tests; funded small rewards. |
| Bank rewards or POS alternatives are better value | High | Merchant comparisons and paid commitments at actual proposed price. |
| Support/fees exceed tiny spread | High | Record actual minutes and charges; reduce manual work or reprice before scaling. |
| Refunds, closures, or currency changes break accounting | High | Explicit policies, matched journal entries, rate snapshots, reconciliation and exit exercises. |

## Launch gates

Proceed to discovery immediately. Proceed to a real-money earned-rewards pilot only after legal/payment classification, funded arrangements, contracts, transaction verification, tenant protection, and reconciliation are ready. Proceed to customer top-ups only after separate permission/partner coverage and positive economics. Proceed to prize campaigns only after exact campaign review and fully available prize funding.

A negative answer does not necessarily end the menu business. Alternatives include menu SaaS with per-shop loyalty, a partnership using an existing rewards provider, or a software-only service while a licensed partner operates value movement. Compare economics and customer ownership before selecting that route.
