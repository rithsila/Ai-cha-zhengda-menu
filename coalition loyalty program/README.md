# Cambodia coalition loyalty business pack

Research date: 9 September 2026. Geography: Cambodia first; proposed pilot in one Phnom Penh neighborhood. Status: decision draft, not a validated business or permission to operate regulated services.

## The business in one sentence

Sell digital menu software to independent cafés and restaurants, and offer an optional shared rewards network where the shop awarding coins funds the reward, another shop can fulfill it, and the platform earns a disclosed service margin.

## Findings that change the plan

- **There is a plausible niche, but this is not an empty market.** Wingpoints and Sathapana Rewards already offer cross-merchant rewards; details are in the competitor report. A shared coin alone is insufficient differentiation.
- **Subscriptions matter at small scale.** Under the illustrative base assumptions, each shop contributes $23.40/month before fixed costs, including a $15 menu subscription. Without that subscription, contribution falls to $8.40. These are model results, not observed earnings.
- **Start with merchant-funded earned rewards.** Preserve customer coin purchases, the $50 Gold route, and prizes as later options subject to commercial validation and local advice. This is a proposed launch sequence, not removal of the founder's vision.
- **The prize idea needs fresh Cambodia-specific review.** A June 2026 beverage instruction creates a material question for this café-focused product. See the [GDT instruction listing](https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/instruction/page/0) and the scoped analysis in report 05. Do not assume that other shops' promotions establish permission.
- **The existing app is a starting point, not a ready coalition platform.** Sampled code contains loyalty, Gold, and prize concepts, but its inspected schema does not establish tenant isolation or financial settlement records.

## Recommended next decision

Approve a discovery budget and interview 15 merchants and 30 customers before committing to a full rebuild. Aim for five merchants willing to sign a conditional pilot agreement with real reward funding. Choose the pilot neighborhood based on where the founder can personally support those shops.

Use the economics file to replace assumptions with actual shop sales, bank fees, software costs, and willingness to pay. A launch date should follow the evidence and legal/payment readiness, not the illustrative 90-day schedule.

## Terms used here

**Coin:** an internal rewards unit; no blockchain, investment return, trading, or customer cash-out is proposed. **Reward funding:** money covering future merchant reimbursement. **Spread:** funding/service receipts less reward obligations, before other costs. **Settlement:** paying a merchant for valid redemptions. **Tenant:** one merchant business, potentially with multiple branches. **Gold:** a membership status, separate from coins.

All proposed prices, volumes, thresholds, budgets, and pilot success targets are assumptions for testing. No merchant, regulator, bank, or customer was contacted. No application code was changed.
