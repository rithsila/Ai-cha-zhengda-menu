## Method and confidence

Searched local bank rewards, telecom VIP programs, chain café rewards, Cambodian merchant software, discovery apps, payment regulation, and sales/prize promotions. Prioritized official operators and Cambodian authorities. Used professional legal/tax summaries where original English interpretation was unavailable. Did not rely on Reddit or unattributed market forecasts.

**High confidence:** named providers publicly advertise the cited features; the GDT lists the 2026 instruction; the sampled repository contains the stated models/functions.

**Medium confidence:** competitive relevance and the proposed neighborhood positioning. These are analytical judgments based on public product descriptions.

**Unproven:** merchant willingness to pay, active competitor usage, attainable transaction volume, customer retention, actual bank fees, integration access, staffing costs, prize ROI, exact legal classification, and unit economics in operation.

No claim is made that this list includes every Cambodian competitor. Search results can be incomplete and localized app terms may change. No interviews, mystery shopping, bank demos, regulator consultation, or certified legal translations were performed. Company claims of scale are not independently audited here.

## Important corrections to early brainstorming

- Shared cross-shop loyalty already exists locally; do not market the concept as Cambodia's first.
- Nectar's economics support the inspiration, not a guaranteed margin or a verified Gold/top-up feature.
- Selling coins at their full reimbursement value produces no spread by itself.
- A chain's prepaid card or telecom top-up program is not legal proof for unrelated-shop stored value.
- Other shops offering prizes does not establish permission, especially given the 2026 beverage developments.
- Existing app code is not evidence of production or regulatory readiness.

## What to replace before making a launch commitment

Replace interview pricing with signed merchant terms; cost allowances with provider quotes; hypothetical sales with anonymized shop records; assumed retention with pilot cohorts; legal questions with written local advice; and software readiness assumptions with tested flows. Keep this research date visible when sharing the pack.
