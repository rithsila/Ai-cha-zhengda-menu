# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Customers browsing the menu to place orders quickly inside Telegram. They need an efficient, frictionless way to find what they want and checkout on mobile.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a Telegram Mini App menu for a dual-brand storefront (Ai-Cha and Zhengda Chicken Steak). It allows users to browse items across both brands seamlessly, customize orders, and checkout directly within Telegram. Success is measured by speed, ease of navigation between the two menus, and a native-feeling mobile experience.

## Positioning
Order from two brands in one tap — no app download, no leaving Telegram.

## Brand Personality
Playful, vibrant, and approachable (like a standard food/boba app). It should feel lively but remain highly functional.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, desktop-first web designs that are hard to tap on mobile.
- Overly serious or sterile enterprise UI (avoid gray/corporate aesthetic).
- Heavy, slow-loading pages that break the instant feel of a Telegram Mini App.

## Design Principles
- **Speed is a feature:** Load instantly and feel extremely responsive.
- **Native Telegram feel:** Seamlessly adapt to Telegram's light/dark mode and respect safe areas.
- **Clarity over cleverness:** Make the ordering flow foolproof and obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Large, easy-to-tap touch targets (minimum 44x44px) for mobile.
- Seamlessly match Telegram's native light/dark mode settings via CSS variables provided by the Telegram Web App API.
- Fast loading for poor network conditions (mobile-first).
